# Systems and Changes

Back to [[Set 19 Summary]]

> [!warning] Pre-release (PBE) info
> Collected Nov 24, 2026. Numbers can change before patch 19.1 on Dec 1.

## Unlocks are now permanent
- The unlock system from Set 16 (Lore & Legends) is now a **permanent TFT feature**, not just a one-set gimmick.
- Full list: [[Unlocks]].

## 7-cost champions
- Four 7-costs, all locked behind hard conditions: [[Unlocks#Baron Nashor|Baron Nashor]], [[Unlocks#Rakan|Rakan]], [[Unlocks#Smeech|Smeech]], [[Unlocks#Sona|Sona]].
- **Baron Nashor** isn't in the normal pool: play Malzahar, buy the Mystical Instruments that show up in your shop, collect all five.
- **Sona** adapts to the five major bands and turns all her stats into AP.
- **Smeech** (Jazz) makes random beats every few seconds; saved-up beats create emblems for traits you're not running.
- There are also 23 different 5-cost units.

## Prismatic traits come from quests, not emblems
- Picking a special Prismatic Tour augment gives you a quest; finishing it unlocks that band's Prismatic tier.
- Works for: [[Pentakill]], [[Ionic Spark]], [[True Damage]], [[Heartsteel]], [[K-DA|K/DA]].

## Augments
- Roughly 330–363 augments depending on the source (the most ever), including six Hero Augments.
- Hero augment examples: Annie riding Tibbers and throwing fireballs, Lillia jumping into the backline to poison, Renekton sleeping through the first half of combat.
- **Blind Box II**: a random Prismatic augment for 8 gold.

## Items
- Emblems still exist for each band/class (give the holder that trait).
- Standard utility items return: artifacts, component anvils, Magnetic Item Removers, Training Gloves, Thief's Gloves.

## Music system
- The soundtrack changes live with your board: 24 genres from 14 bands and 10 solo artists.
- Each genre has melody, harmony and bassline layers that mix — 10,000+ combinations.
- Hitting a higher trait breakpoint upgrades that genre's track. Planning phase is calmer; combat is louder.
- Solo artists play solos over the band, or take the lead.

## Client and rewards
- TFT has its own standalone client with missions that give rewards for playing.
- Play 1 Set 19 game → 500 Treasure Tokens. Play 20 → a random Mythic Chibi.
- **Tour Collection**: collect Prismatic Tour souvenirs to unlock portals, little legends and room decorations.
- New **Exalted** cosmetic tier, starting with Exalted Chibi Ionic Spark Gwen. Ionic Spark arena, Aurora and Briar chibis.
