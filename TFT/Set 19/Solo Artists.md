# Solo Artists (unique traits)

Back to [[Set 19 Summary]] · [[Traits Overview]]

Single-champion origins with their own music layer. Solo artists can take over a band's song.

## 8-Bit
**[[Champions#Miss Fortune|Miss Fortune]]** · 5-cost

Tracks team damage; Miss Fortune's damage counts triple. Beat the high score to earn a prize — her quest is to keep breaking records.

## Beat Master
**[[Champions#Rell|Rell]]** · 5-cost

Mounts up at a Health threshold, heals over time and fires magic line attacks. Pairs with Mel in [[R&B]].

## Boy Band
**[[Champions#Rakan|Rakan]]** · 7-cost

Summons clones that scale with his Health and copy his items. 7-cost — see [[Unlocks#Rakan|unlock]].

## Composer
**[[Champions#Malzahar|Malzahar]]** · 3-cost

Mystical Instruments boost Ability Power and show up in your shop while you play Malzahar. Collect all five and deal enough damage with Malzahar to unlock [[Unlocks#Baron Nashor|Baron Nashor]].

## Country
**[[Champions#Alistar|Alistar]]** · 4-cost

Gains Durability at combat start if he has enough bonus Health. See [[Unlocks#Alistar|unlock]].

## Disco
**[[Champions#Nunu & Willump|Nunu & Willump]]** · 5-cost

Heals the team over time; casting abilities grants team Attack Speed.

## Emo
**[[Champions#Amumu|Amumu]]** · 3-cost

Gains Armor and Magic Resist if he starts combat with no adjacent allies.

## Freestyle
**[[Champions#Akali (True Damage)|Akali (True Damage)]]** · 5-cost

Passive switches between generating gold and restoring Player Health.

## Goth
**[[Champions#Zeri|Zeri]]** · 5-cost

Her first damaging hit each combat steals Attack Damage from the enemy.

## Hype Man
**[[Champions#Teemo|Teemo]]** · 5-cost

Hyperpop champions store Hype when they deal or take damage.

## Indie
**[[Champions#Seraphine|Seraphine]]** · 2-cost

Grants random champions after combat and gets stronger as you 3-star champions (Rising Star → Superstar forms).

## Mariachi
**[[Champions#Nidalee|Nidalee]]** · 5-cost

Switches between Cougar Form (pounces) and Human Form (healing, flowers) based on Health.

## Melodic Heart
**[[Champions#Gwen|Gwen]]** · 5-cost

The strongest Gwen is shielded by Mist at combat start; allies in the Mist gain Durability.

## One Monster Band
**[[Champions#Baron Nashor|Baron Nashor]]** · 7-cost

Takes two board slots, is unstoppable, steals enemy stats after his first cast, and summons an orchestra and a Rift Herald.

## Producer
**[[Champions#Sona|Sona]]** · 7-cost

Gains a chosen band's trait and converts her bonus AD and Attack Speed into AP.

## Record Mogul
**[[Champions#Dr. Mundo|Dr. Mundo]]** · 5-cost

Periodic Record Contracts grant Damage Amp; kills give Dr. Mundo permanent Health.

## Synthwave
**[[Champions#Garen|Garen]]** · 4-cost

Gains a Shield at a Health threshold, based on healing and shielding received.

## Tango
**[[Champions#Twisted Fate|Twisted Fate]]** · 5-cost

Pairs with an ally to grant Attack Speed, Burn and Wound.

## Windblessed
**[[Champions#Janna|Janna]]** · 5-cost

Her tornado knocks up enemies at combat start.
