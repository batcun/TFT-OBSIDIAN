## How unlocks work
- 32 of 94 champions start locked. Meet the condition → a copy is guaranteed in your next shop, and the champion stays in your pool all game.
- Some need a minimum player level.
- Three kinds: **Flex** (fit many boards, e.g. Vayne), **Capstone** (mostly 5-costs that finish deep trait comps), **Chase** (big challenges, e.g. Baron Nashor).
- The two Akalis are exclusive: unlocking one blocks the other.
