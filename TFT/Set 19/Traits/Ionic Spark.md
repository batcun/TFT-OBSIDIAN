# Ionic Spark

**Band** · Breakpoints: **3 / 5 / 7 / 10** · Back to [[Set 19 Summary]] · [[Traits Overview]]

## What it does
- Fans send gifts and grant Health during combat at 3 and 5.
- At 7, consumables give permanent buffs every two rounds — they stay even if you drop below 7.
- Higher tiers add gold, consumables and a **stage rush at 10**. Reaches a Prismatic tier.
- Unlocks tied to it: [[Unlocks#Tibbers|Tibbers]] (via Annie), [[Unlocks#Jinx|Jinx]], [[Unlocks#Gwen|Gwen]].
