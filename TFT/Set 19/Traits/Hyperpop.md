# Hyperpop

**Band** · Breakpoints: **2 / 3 / 4 / 5** · Back to [[Set 19 Summary]] · [[Traits Overview]]

## What it does
- All-Yordle band. Gains Health + Attack Speed and builds **Hype** after combat (more for takedowns); Hype pays out loot.
- Unlock chain: Ziggs (only non-locked member) → [[Unlocks#Kobuko|Kobuko]] → [[Unlocks#Lulu|Lulu]] / [[Unlocks#Tristana|Tristana]] → [[Unlocks#Teemo|Teemo]] at 900 Hype.
- Kobuko grows with Hype. Teemo's solo trait [[Solo Artists#Hype Man|Hype Man]] stores Hype when dealing/taking damage.
