# Gu Feng

**Band** · Breakpoints: **2 / 3 / 4** · Back to [[Set 19 Summary]] · [[Traits Overview]]

## What it does
- Champions gain stats that scale with **player level**.
- At 4, pick a Scroll: **Heavenly Prayer** (becomes a radiant item at level 10) or **the Beast** (huge AD/AP/mana regen and a path to **level 11**, but you're devoured after 2 combat losses in a row).
- Every unlock needs Xayah on your board: [[Unlocks#Wukong|Wukong]], [[Unlocks#Janna|Janna]], [[Unlocks#Lee Sin|Lee Sin]].
