# K/DA

**Band** · Breakpoints: **3 / 5 / 7 / 10 / 13 / 15 / 21 (total star levels)** · Back to [[Set 19 Summary]] · [[Traits Overview]]

## What it does
- Counts **total star levels** of K/DA champions, so starring up cheap members scales it.
- Song breakpoints: POP/STARS (lower mana) → DRUM GO DUM (shield) → VILLAIN (attack speed) → I'LL SHOW YOU (boosts earlier effects) → FIRESTARTER (burn).
- Prismatic breakpoint unlocks Evelynn and Akali; top tier ascends members to 4-star. Leona is the band's manager.
- Unlocks: [[Unlocks#Evelynn|Evelynn]], [[Unlocks#Akali (K-DA)|K/DA Akali]] (only if True Damage Akali isn't unlocked). Reaches a Prismatic tier.
