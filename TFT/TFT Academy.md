## The TFT Academy overlay
- Its page says it gives **real-time comp guidance, item builds and positioning tips during games**, plus match recording and review after.
- The page doesn't say whether it's Riot-approved (only that TFT Academy isn't endorsed by Riot).
- Riot's TFT rules don't allow apps that give real-time info or make decisions for you, unless Riot has approved them. **Before using it in ranked, check that it's a Riot-approved app.** Its after-game review features are the safe part.
