- **Theme:** music. Brings back the Set 10 music theme plus the Unlock mechanic from Set 16, which is now permanent.

## Quick things to remember
- Only **Ziggs** is unlocked in Hyperpop at the start; each Yordle unlocks the next, ending at Teemo (900 Hype).
- **Gu Feng** unlocks all need **Xayah** on your board.
- **K/DA** counts total *star levels*, not unit count, so 2-starring cheap members matters.
- **True Damage**: you can flip between A-Side (win streak) and B-Side (loss streak) mid-game.
- The two **Akalis** block each other: unlock one and the other can't be unlocked.
- **Jazz** + [[Unlocks#Smeech|Smeech]] reward running lots of different traits.
